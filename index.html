What's inside

Everything your group planned, in one place.

Join with a six-letter code or your leader's QR, and the app fills itself with your group's days, places and people.

Today, at a glance

Departures, buses, tent moves, meals and talks on one timeline, with your leader's notes pinned where you will see them. The full itinerary covers 8 to 13 Dhul Hijjah.

Prayer times and Qibla

Accurate times for wherever you are, a Qibla compass, a note on shortened prayers while travelling, and optional reminders before each prayer.

Check-in and headcount

When your leader opens a check-in, one tap says you are on the bus or at the tent. See the live headcount and who is still on the way.

Emergency, one hold away

Hold SOS for three seconds to alert your leader with your position. Call your leader or an ambulance directly. "Guide me back" points you to your tent.

Your documents

Passport, permit, visa, vaccination and insurance in one place, with a checkpoint view to show at a glance. You choose which staff can see each one.

Guide and duas

The rites of Hajj step by step, tracked as you go, with a dua for each day in Arabic and your language.

Activities and social

See what your group has organised, from lectures to shared meals, and RSVP so the organisers know you are coming.

Notifications

Messages from your leader and reminders from the app, grouped by day, so nothing is missed.

Offline first

Built for the holy sites

Your schedule, documents, prayer times and medical card are on the phone, not behind a signal. Changes you make offline are sent when you reconnect.

Private by design

Location is yours to share

Location sharing is off unless you turn it on, and the moment you turn it off, every position we held is deleted. An SOS sends your position once, and says so.

Two languages

English and Arabic

Fully translated, with right-to-left layout, and Arabic numerals where you expect them.
